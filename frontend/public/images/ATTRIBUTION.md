# Product photo credits

The active catalog includes real photographs of assembled mecha model kits and their parts. The catalog clearly labels them as reference photos because they may not match the fictional sample product names or exact products. Football photographs below are legacy assets and are no longer used by the active catalog.

The product photographs below are stored locally so the storefront does not depend on a third-party image server. Each image remains under its source license.

These are representative reference photographs, not guarantees of the exact color or model sold. Images were resized to catalog dimensions; no cropping or other edits were made.
